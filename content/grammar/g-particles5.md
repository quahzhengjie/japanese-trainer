Particles come **after** a word and mark its job in the sentence.

| Particle | Role | Example |
|---|---|---|
| は (wa) | topic — "as for…" | わたし**は** にほんじんです |
| が | subject — identifies who/what | だれ**が** きますか |
| を (o) | direct object | パン**を** たべます |
| に | destination, place of existence, time | がっこう**に** いきます／７じ**に** おきます |
| で | place of an action, means | レストラン**で** たべます／バス**で** いきます |

Key contrasts:
- **に vs で** — に marks where something *is* or *goes*; で marks where an
  action *happens*.
- **は vs が** — は sets the topic (often old information); が points out the
  subject (often new, or the answer to a question).
