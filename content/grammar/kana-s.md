The S row — watch the second one: し is **shi**, not "si".

| Kana | Rōmaji |
|---|---|
| さ | sa |
| し | shi |
| す | su |
| せ | se |
| そ | so |

That single irregular reading (し = shi) is the only thing to watch in this row.
