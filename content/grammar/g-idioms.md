N1 expects familiarity with **kotowaza** (proverbs) and **yojijukugo**
(four-character idiomatic compounds). Both are fixed — the wording does not
change.

**Kotowaza (ことわざ) — proverbs**

| Proverb | Literal | Meaning |
|---|---|---|
| 猿も木から落ちる | Even monkeys fall from trees | Even experts make mistakes |
| 七転び八起き | Fall seven times, rise eight | Persevere through failure |
| 石の上にも三年 | Three years on a stone | Patience eventually pays off |
| 急がば回れ | If in a hurry, go around | The safe slow way is fastest |
| 花より団子 | Dumplings over flowers | Substance over appearance |
| 二兎を追う者は一兎をも得ず | Chase two hares, catch none | Trying for too much yields nothing |
| 塵も積もれば山となる | Dust piled up becomes a mountain | Small things add up |

**Yojijukugo (四字熟語) — four-character compounds**

| Compound | Reading | Meaning |
|---|---|---|
| 一石二鳥 | いっせきにちょう | One stone, two birds |
| 自業自得 | じごうじとく | You reap what you sow |
| 十人十色 | じゅうにんといろ | To each their own; many tastes |
| 一期一会 | いちごいちえ | A once-in-a-lifetime encounter |
| 温故知新 | おんこちしん | Learn the new by studying the old |
| 臨機応変 | りんきおうへん | Adapting flexibly to circumstances |
| 我田引水 | がでんいんすい | Doing things to one's own advantage |
| 半信半疑 | はんしんはんぎ | Half believing, half doubting |
| 言語道断 | ごんごどうだん | Outrageous; beyond words |
| 油断大敵 | ゆだんたいてき | Carelessness is the great enemy |

Tip: many yojijukugo are two paired two-character words (半信＋半疑); spotting
the pairing helps you guess the meaning.
