---
id: kana-youon
title: Hiragana — yōon (contracted sounds)
level: N5
tags: [hiragana, kana]
---
**Yōon** are made by writing a *small* や, ゆ or よ after an *i*-column kana
(き, し, ち, に, ひ, み, り and their voiced forms). The two kana are read as **one
syllable**.

| Base | + ゃ | + ゅ | + ょ |
|---|---|---|---|
| き | きゃ kya | きゅ kyu | きょ kyo |
| し | しゃ sha | しゅ shu | しょ sho |
| ち | ちゃ cha | ちゅ chu | ちょ cho |
| に | にゃ nya | にゅ nyu | にょ nyo |
| ひ | ひゃ hya | ひゅ hyu | ひょ hyo |

The や/ゆ/よ is written **small**. Compare きや (ki-ya, two syllables) with
きゃ (kya, one syllable). し, ち and じ drop the *y*: しゃ = sha, じゃ = ja.
