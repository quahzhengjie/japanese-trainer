N2 also tests fixed expressions and collocations — phrases whose meaning
you cannot fully work out from the individual words. Learn them as units.

| Expression | Meaning |
|---|---|
| ～ものの | although; even though |
| ～ところに / ～ところへ | just when; right as |
| ～につれて | as ~ (progresses), accordingly |
| ～にしたがって | in accordance with; as |
| ～をはじめ | starting with; including |
| ～を込めて | with (feeling) put into it |
| ～をめぐって | concerning; over (an issue) |
| ～に関して | regarding; with respect to |
| ～にあたって | on the occasion of |
| ～ぬきで | without; leaving out |
| ～気味 | a touch of; slightly |
| ～がたい | hard to ~ |

**Examples**

- 約束した**ものの**、行きたくない。 — *Although* I promised, I don't want to go.
- 出かけ**ようとした**ところに、電話が鳴った。 — *Just as* I was about to leave, the phone rang.
- 年を取る**につれて**、体が弱くなる。 — *As* you age, your body grows weaker.
- 校長先生**をはじめ**、多くの先生が来た。 — Many teachers came, *starting with* the principal.
- 心**を込めて**手紙を書いた。 — I wrote the letter *with all my heart*.
- 最近、風邪**気味**だ。 — Lately I have *a touch of* a cold.
- 彼の態度は理解し**がたい**。 — His attitude is *hard to* understand.

～につれて and ～にしたがって are close in meaning; ～にしたがって can also
mean "following an instruction/rule", which ～につれて cannot.
