title: Katakana — vowels through the T row
level: N5

---
**Katakana** has the same sounds as hiragana but a sharper, more angular shape.
It is used for foreign loanwords, names and emphasis.

| Vowels | K row | S row | T row |
|---|---|---|---|
| ア a | カ ka | サ sa | タ ta |
| イ i | キ ki | シ shi | チ chi |
| ウ u | ク ku | ス su | ツ tsu |
| エ e | ケ ke | セ se | テ te |
| オ o | コ ko | ソ so | ト to |

Watch the look-alikes: **シ shi** vs **ツ tsu**, and **ソ so** vs **ン n** — the
difference is stroke direction. The reading rules are identical to hiragana
(シ = shi, チ = chi, ツ = tsu).
