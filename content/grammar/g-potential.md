title: Potential form — can do

The **potential form** expresses ability or possibility — "can do" / "be able
to do".

**How to form it**

| Verb type | Rule | Example |
|---|---|---|
| u-verb (G1) | final -u → -eru | のむ → のめる、 かく → かける |
| ru-verb (G2) | drop る, add られる | たべる → たべられる |
| する | → できる | べんきょうする → べんきょうできる |
| くる | → こられる | — |

In casual modern speech, ru-verbs often drop the ら: たべられる → たべれる
("ら-nuki"). This is common but considered informal.

**Particle change**

The object marker を usually becomes **が** with potential verbs:

- にほんごを はなします。→ にほんご**が** はなせます。
- I can speak Japanese.

**Examples**

- かんじが **よめます**。— I can read kanji.
- あした **こられません**。— I can't come tomorrow.
- ここで しゃしんが **とれます**か。— Can I take photos here?

The verb できる is itself the potential of する and is used very widely on its
own: りょうりが できます (I can cook).
