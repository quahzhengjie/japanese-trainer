title: Questions — か and question words
level: N5

To make a question, add **か** to the end of a sentence. No change in word order,
no question mark needed.

> がくせいです → がくせいです**か**。 — Are you a student?

**Question words** replace the unknown part of the sentence:

| Word | Meaning |
|---|---|
| なに / なん | what |
| だれ | who |
| どこ | where |
| いつ | when |
| いくら | how much |
| どう | how |

- これは **なん**ですか。 — What is this?
- **だれ**が きますか。 — Who is coming?
- えきは **どこ**ですか。 — Where is the station?
- たんじょうびは **いつ**ですか。 — When is your birthday?

Answer with the same structure, swapping the question word for the real
information. なに becomes **なん** before です and counters (なんですか, なんじ).
