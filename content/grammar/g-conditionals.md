# Conditionals — と, ば, たら, なら

Japanese has four "if/when" forms. They overlap, but each has a core use.

## Quick comparison

| Form | Core meaning | Second clause restriction |
|---|---|---|
| **と** | automatic / natural result, general truth | NO command, request, invitation, or intention |
| **ば** | hypothetical condition ("if") | Avoid command if subjects differ |
| **たら** | "if / when" — flexible, also "and then" | Almost no restriction |
| **なら** | "if it's the case that…" — advice on a raised topic | Free |

## と — natural consequence

Attach to the **dictionary form**. The result always follows automatically.

- はるに なる**と**、さくらが さきます。 — When spring comes, cherry blossoms bloom.
- この ボタンを おす**と**、きっぷが でます。 — Press this button and a ticket comes out.

✗ あした はれる**と**、いきましょう。 *(と cannot lead to an invitation — use たら.)*

## ば — hypothetical "if"

| Word type | ば form |
|---|---|
| Group 1 verb | final -u → -e + ば: いく → い**けば** |
| Group 2 verb | drop る + れば: たべる → たべ**れば** |
| い-adjective | drop い + ければ: やすい → やす**ければ** |
| な-adj / noun | + なら(ば): しずか**ならば** |

- やすけれ**ば**、かいます。 — If it's cheap, I'll buy it.
- お金が あれ**ば**、りょこうする。 — If I had money, I'd travel.

## たら — "if / when," and "and then"

Attach to the **plain past** form (＋ら): いく → いった → いった**ら**.

- うちに ついた**ら**、でんわして ください。 — When you get home, please call.
- えきに ついた**ら**、でんしゃは でて いた。 — When I reached the station, the train had (already) left. *(discovery)*

## なら — advice on a topic just raised

- 「りょこうに いきたい。」「りょこう**なら**、おきなわが いいよ。」 — "I want to travel." "If it's travel, Okinawa is good."

なら responds to what the other person just said and is the natural choice for **recommendations**.
