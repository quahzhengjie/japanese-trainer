title: The polite ます form
level: N5
tags: [grammar, verbs]
---
The **ます form** is the polite present/future of a verb. It also doubles as the
future — context tells you which.

| Meaning | Ending | Example (たべる — eat) |
|---|---|---|
| do / will do | ます | たべ**ます** |
| do not | ません | たべ**ません** |
| did | ました | たべ**ました** |
| did not | ませんでした | たべ**ませんでした** |

- まいにち コーヒーを のみ**ます**。 — I drink coffee every day.
- きのう テレビを み**ました**。 — I watched TV yesterday.
- にくを たべ**ません**。 — I don't eat meat.

The ます form is the safe, polite default — use it with people you don't know
well. The negative past **ませんでした** is one of the longest endings; practise
it as a chunk.
