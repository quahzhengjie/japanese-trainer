These patterns are even more **literary or emphatic**. Several are fixed
expressions whose conjugation does not change.

| Pattern | Connects to | Meaning |
|---|---|---|
| ～まじき | verb (dict.) | unbecoming; must not be done (moral) |
| ～んがため(に) | verb (ない-stem) | for the very purpose of |
| ～ずくめ | noun | completely full of; nothing but |
| ～きらいがある | noun の／verb (dict.) | tends (undesirably) to ~ |
| ～ともなると／ともなれば | noun / verb | once it gets to ~; when it comes to ~ |
| ～(よ)うが／(よ)うと | verb volitional | no matter whether ~ |
| ～たりとも | counter + one | not even a single ~ (～ない follows) |
| ～であれ／であろうと | noun | whether it be ~; even if ~ |
| ～が最後 | verb (た-form) | once ~ happens, (bad result) |
| ～の極み | noun | the height/extreme of ~ |

**Examples**

- 教師にあるまじき言動だ。 — Conduct unbecoming of a teacher.
- 生き延びんがために必死で働いた。 — He worked desperately in order to survive.
- 今年はいいことずくめだった。 — This year was nothing but good things.
- 彼は物事を悪く考えるきらいがある。 — He tends to think badly of things.
- 専門家ともなると説明が違う。 — Once you are an expert, the explanation differs.
- 一日たりとも休めない。 — I cannot rest even for a single day.
- 何が起ころうと驚かない。 — No matter what happens, I won't be surprised.

する verbs take せん before んがため: 成功せんがため. ～が最後 always carries a
negative, irreversible consequence.
