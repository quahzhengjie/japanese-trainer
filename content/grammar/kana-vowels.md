Every Japanese syllable is built on five vowel sounds. Learn these first — every
other kana is a consonant joined to one of them.

| Kana | Rōmaji | Sounds like |
|---|---|---|
| あ | a | "ah" |
| い | i | "ee" |
| う | u | "oo" |
| え | e | "eh" |
| お | o | "oh" |

The order **a · i · u · e · o** is fixed and used everywhere — memorise it as a chant.
