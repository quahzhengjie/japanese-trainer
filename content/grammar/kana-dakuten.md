title: Hiragana — dakuten and handakuten
level: N5
tags: [hiragana, kana]
---
Two small marks change a kana's consonant. **Dakuten** ( ゛ ) voices it;
**handakuten** ( ゜ ), used only on the H row, makes a *p* sound.

| Plain | + dakuten | + handakuten |
|---|---|---|
| か k → | が g | — |
| さ s → | ざ z | — |
| た t → | だ d | — |
| は h → | ば b | ぱ p |

So the row さ し す せ そ becomes ざ じ ず ぜ ぞ, and は ひ ふ へ ほ can become
ば び ぶ べ ぼ **or** ぱ ぴ ぷ ぺ ぽ.

Watch the irregular readings: じ = **ji**, ぢ = **ji**, づ = **zu**.
