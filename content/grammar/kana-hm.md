Two more rows: the *h* sounds and the *m* sounds.

**H row**

| Kana | Rōmaji |
|---|---|
| は | ha |
| ひ | hi |
| ふ | fu |
| へ | he |
| ほ | ho |

Note ふ is **fu**, not *hu* — the lips barely touch.

**M row**

| Kana | Rōmaji |
|---|---|
| ま | ma |
| み | mi |
| む | mu |
| め | me |
| も | mo |

は and へ have special readings (**wa** and **e**) when used as particles — but as
plain kana they are *ha* and *he*.
