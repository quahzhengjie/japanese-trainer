The T row has **two** irregular readings: ち is **chi** and つ is **tsu**.

| Kana | Rōmaji |
|---|---|
| た | ta |
| ち | chi |
| つ | tsu |
| て | te |
| と | to |

ち (chi) and つ (tsu) are the tricky ones — say them aloud a few times.
