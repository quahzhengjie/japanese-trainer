N1 grammar leans heavily on **formal, written and set patterns**. Many appear in
news writing, business documents and speeches rather than casual speech.

| Pattern | Connects to | Meaning |
|---|---|---|
| ～をもって | noun | with / by means of; as of (a date) — formal |
| ～ならではの | noun | distinctive of; only possible for ~ |
| ～べく | verb (dict.) | in order to; with the aim of |
| ～や否や / ～が早いか | verb (dict.) | the moment that; as soon as |
| ～にあって | noun | in (a situation/era); being in ~ |
| ～をよそに | noun | ignoring; in disregard of |
| ～をおいて | noun | apart from; other than (～ない follows) |
| ～にひきかえ | noun | in contrast to; unlike |
| ～にもまして | noun | even more than ~ |
| ～いかんで／いかんによっては | noun | depending on ~ |

**Examples**

- 本日をもって閉店いたします。 — We close as of today.
- この味は老舗ならではのものだ。 — This flavour is something only a long-established shop can offer.
- 真相を究明すべく調査を続ける。 — They continue the inquiry in order to uncover the truth.
- 駅に着くや否や電車が出てしまった。 — The instant I reached the station, the train left.
- 非常時にあって冷静さが求められる。 — In an emergency, composure is demanded.
- 彼をおいて適任者はいない。 — There is no suitable person other than him.

Note: ～をもって often pairs with formal verbs (いたします, とする). ～ならではの
is followed by a noun; ～ならでは（だ） can also close a sentence.
