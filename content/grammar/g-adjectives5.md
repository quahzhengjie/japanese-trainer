title: い-adjectives and な-adjectives
level: N5

---
Japanese has **two kinds of adjectives**, and they behave differently.

**い-adjectives** end in い and change their own ending.

| Form | Example (たかい — expensive) |
|---|---|
| plain | たかい |
| negative | たか**くない** |
| past | たか**かった** |
| before a noun | たかい くるま |

> このカメラは **たかいです**。 — This camera is expensive.

**な-adjectives** take **な** when placed before a noun, and use です/じゃ
ありません like a noun.

| Form | Example (げんき — healthy/lively) |
|---|---|
| before a noun | げんき**な** ひと |
| is | げんき**です** |
| is not | げんき**じゃありません** |

> きれい**な** はな。 / このへやは しずか**です**。

Note: いい (good) is irregular — negative is **よくない**, not *いくない*.
