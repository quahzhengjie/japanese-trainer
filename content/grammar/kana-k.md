The K row is each vowel with a *k* sound in front of it.

| Kana | Rōmaji |
|---|---|
| か | ka |
| き | ki |
| く | ku |
| け | ke |
| こ | ko |

Later you will add two small marks to turn か into **が** (ga) — but learn the
plain row first.
